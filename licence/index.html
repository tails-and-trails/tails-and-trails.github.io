What travels with each file

  • A stable archive sequence and filename
  • File-level attribution in the public registry
  • A SHA-256 identifier on the catalogue record
  • Independent GitHub and IPFS download links

Privacy-preserving public copies

The published photographs exclude original camera EXIF, device and exact capture-location metadata. The locality coordinate describes Ericeira generally and is not an exact capture location.
